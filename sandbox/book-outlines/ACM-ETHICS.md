This page maps the data-science-lifecycle chapters of the Ethics in Data Science
book to relevant competencies from the ACM *Competencies for Data Science* (a
continuation of the ACM Data Science Task Force's *Computing Competencies for
Undergraduate Data Science Curricula*, 2021).

> **Note on sourcing.** The ACM's formal *competency statements* are still under
> development ("*Competency statements are under development and will be
> available in the next public release*"). The citable, released content is the
> set of **Knowledge Units** and their **knowledge elements** (the Core /
> Specialization / Elective bullets). Each bullet below quotes a knowledge
> element **verbatim** from the ACM document and labels it with its Knowledge
> Area (KA) — Knowledge Unit (KU) so the mapping is traceable to the source.
