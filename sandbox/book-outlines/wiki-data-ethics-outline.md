# Big Data Ethics (Wikipedia)
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Big_data_ethics

* Definition: ethical issues around collection, use, and governance of large/commercial personal data sets

* Core principles
  * Ownership — individuals control personal data
  * Transparency — clear visibility into data use and algorithms
  * Consent — informed, explicit permission for data use
  * Privacy — protect individuals from harmful disclosure
  * Currency — awareness of economic value derived from data
  * Openness — appropriate sharing of aggregate/open datasets

* Key concerns
  * Algorithmic bias and opaque "black box" models
  * Repurposing/control creep of data
  * Predictive analytics and anticipatory governance (targeting, profiling)
  * Government surveillance and disproportionate harms
  * Trade-offs between privacy and research/public benefit

* Valuation & openness
  * Economic questions: how much is personal data worth?
  * Models: data markets vs. open-data movements
  * Variation in willingness to share across populations

* Illustrative events
  * Snowden disclosures — mass surveillance, policy impact
  * Cambridge Analytica — consent, repurposing, political targeting

* Practical responses
  * Technical/reporting tools: datasheets, model cards
  * Ethical frameworks, toolkits, and policy/regulatory approaches (e.g., GDPR)

* See also: ethics of AI, information ethics, data justice
