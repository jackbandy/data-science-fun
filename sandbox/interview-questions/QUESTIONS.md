# Data Science Interview Question PDFs

These PDFs were downloaded from public URLs on June 8, 2026. The Microsoft
and Chan Zuckerberg Initiative guides are first-party. The other PDFs are
third-party compilations, so their claims that a question was asked by a
company have not been independently verified.

For large general question banks, this index lists the core interview prompts
and omits answer text, repeated variants, and general career-advice questions.

## `czi-official-data-science-interview-guide.pdf`

Source: <https://chanzuckerberg.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Data-Science-Interview-Guide.pdf>

This is a first-party Chan Zuckerberg Initiative candidate guide. It describes
the coding, domain analytics, generalized analytics, and cross-functional
interviews used for data science candidates.

Example questions and exercises:

- Using SQL, R, or Python, ingest and analyze a provided CSV file to answer technical data questions.
- Given student subject and grade data, predict a student's performance on projects.
- Based on the student-performance analysis, what product recommendations would you make?
- Explain how and why you would implement a proposed analytical design.
- A new feature is intended to capture how frequently students work on their projects. As the lead data scientist, what would you measure?
- Which metrics would you track for that feature?
- How would you communicate the feature's results to the rest of the team?
- How would you compare alternative solutions based on time complexity, space complexity, implementation difficulty, and extensibility?
- What edge cases should your solution handle?

## `deep-learning-interviews-open-access.pdf`

Source: <https://arxiv.org/pdf/2201.00650>

This is the open-access second edition of *Deep Learning Interviews* by Shlomo
Kashani. It contains hundreds of solved technical interview and graduate-level
questions. The following is a representative subset:

- What statistical tests can establish the significance of an interaction term?
- Given a logistic-regression observation, what are its logit, odds, and predicted probability?
- What are the explanatory and response variables in a regression problem?
- At what probability does binary entropy attain its maximum?
- What is the relationship between entropy and the logit function?
- Which mathematical function does a numerically stable softmax implementation compute?
- Why is numerical overflow relevant when implementing exponentials?
- What is the intuition behind a Bayesian representation of model uncertainty?
- How do you analytically find the maximum-likelihood estimate of a likelihood function?
- What is the relationship between a likelihood function and a log-likelihood function?
- What is the first derivative of a log-likelihood function called?
- Why does the Central Limit Theorem produce a standard Gaussian after normalization?
- What is the distribution of the sample mean of Bernoulli random variables?
- For a certain event, what is the entropy?
- For N equiprobable events, what is the entropy?
- What is the expression for the Boltzmann probability distribution?
- Is a given dataset linearly separable?
- What is the loss function for linear regression?
- What is the gradient-descent algorithm for minimizing a function?
- What is the hypothesis function in logistic regression?
- What is a logistic-regression decision boundary?
- What does a logistic output of 0.8 mean?
- Which differentiation methods are prone to rounding error?
- What is Monte Carlo dropout?
- How can a pretrained convolutional network be used as a feature extractor?
- Why can freezing pretrained parameters be useful?
- What dimensions result from a valid-padding convolution?
- How many multiplications and additions are required for a convolution?
- What is locality-sensitive hashing?
- Which activation functions cannot be backpropagated through, and why?
- How many hidden layers does a given neural-network architecture have?
- Why can ReLU outperform sigmoidal activation functions?
- What are accuracy, precision, and recall for a supplied confusion matrix?
- What is batch normalization?
- What mathematical process performs normalization?
- What can a convolutional layer learn?
- What can go wrong with a supplied neural-network training pipeline?
- How are overfitting and underfitting reflected in training and validation curves?
- What is the main idea behind early stopping?
- Why can gradient variance increase as batch size decreases?
- What is the most common use of max-pooling layers?
- How does a MaxPool2D operation change tensor shape?
