public replication archive for "Fake news on Twitter during the 2016 U.S. presidential election", Science 2019. For educational purposes only.

## Files

- `twitter-fake-news-replication-v0.6.zip`: Zenodo archive of the `LazerLab/twitter-fake-news-replication` public replication materials, version `v0.6`.
  - compressed size: approximately 21 MB
