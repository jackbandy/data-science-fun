# Checkpoint One - Question Memo

The purpose of this exercise is to help identify and organize project groups around shared interests.

To that end, please share two or three research-oriented questions that you would be interested in exploring for the group project portion of this class. These can be general-interest questions, specific mental itches, or questions from studies that have piqued your interest. Basically, any question that could be addressed with data is acceptable.

## Examples

Here are some examples from previous semesters, which students used to guide their projects:

- How does sleep affect mental health and academic success among college students in the United States?
- How does screen time combined with other digital habits affect a person's mental health?
- What are the leading causes of traffic accidents? Which locations in Chicago have the most traffic accidents?
- Do district-level school spending priorities predict student outcomes?
- Do body-worn cameras reduce use-of-force incidents among police?
- What circumstances are most likely to result in unreported, missing "ghost buses?"
- How does the tone of international media coverage shape understanding of conflicts in Palestine?
- Do major sports games affect short-term crime rate, especially after a loss?
- Do EPL clubs display more long-term dominance than NFL teams?
- How do lifestyle habits, such as diet, exercise, sleep, smoking, alcohol consumption, and stress, affect the risk of heart disease?

## If you need inspiration

The [ideas page](/ideas/) shuffles through different research studies that could give you ideas for questions.

You might also peruse some of the [datasets that are pre-curated for this class](https://github.com/jackbandy/data-science-fun/tree/main/datasets).

Please spend some time on this to really consider what you are interested in. You will be spending much of the semester working on this group project, and these questions will be the best way to make sure you are working on something you enjoy.
