{%- comment -%} Preview-card image tags, shared by _layouts/default.html and index.html. Open Graph plus Twitter Cards is the whole convention -- Facebook, LinkedIn, Slack, Discord, Signal, Telegram, WhatsApp, iMessage, Mastodon, Bluesky, Teams and Pinterest all read one or both. There is no third vocabulary worth carrying. Call it with `primary=true` only where nothing else emits the base tags -- index.html, timer/ and spectogram/, which write their own heads. On {% seo %} pages jekyll-seo-tag already emits og:image, twitter:image, twitter:card and twitter:site, and repeating them here with different values would leave scrapers picking between two answers. Two images are declared, in order: 1. the animated GIF, for readers that show motion (Slack, Discord, Telegram) 2. a 20KB PNG still of the same first frame, for everything with a size ceiling -- WhatsApp in particular drops thumbnails over a few hundred KB, and a 3.9MB GIF is far past that. Most scrapers take the first og:image and ignore the rest; ones that skip what they cannot render fall to the PNG. Either way the picture is the same map.
