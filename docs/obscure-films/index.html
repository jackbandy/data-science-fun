Obscure Films

Every film IMDb users have rated, sorted by how many votes it has (most popular on the left, most obscure on the right). A film at the left edge has more votes than almost every other film; one at the right edge has more votes than almost none. The most-voted 0.1% and the least-voted 0.1% are set aside. Each bar is the total number of votes cast for the films in that 1% band. Drag the two gates to choose a range, then sample eight films from it.
