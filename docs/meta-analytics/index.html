Hapax legomena

Unusual words

Which words does this site use at a rate unlike ordinary English? For every word used at least — times here, its rate — occurrences per million words — is divided by its rate in a large corpus of English. Above 1 the word is over-used, below 1 under-used, and the same number is used for both charts below. — words qualified. Hover a row for the exact figures.

Loading word data…

The baseline is Peter Norvig's word-frequency list of the 333,333 most common English words, published with the "Natural Language Corpus Data" chapter of Beautiful Data (O'Reilly, 2009) and derived from Google's Web 1T corpus (Brants and Franz, LDC2006T13, 2006). This is just a starting point - it's only web pages, so navigation and storefront words like home, contact, free, price, etc. are far more common than in normal English written prose. The "under-used" words are mostly artifacts of the 2006 web. And it is from 2006, so words that postdate it — llms, agentic, github — are absent entirely and are excluded rather than reported as infinitely over-used. Again, will have to look for a better sample at some point... Last built —.
