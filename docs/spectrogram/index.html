Nothing is recorded. The audio is analyzed frame by frame and never leaves your device.

Turn on the microphone, then whistle, or hum, or speak to see the spectrogram in action.

now time → (tick every second)

Each vertical stripe is one slice of sound, about 16 ms wide: a short-time Fourier transform splits that slice into frequencies, and each frequency is inked in proportion to how loud it is. Whistles come out as thin lines, vowels as stacked formants, and a hand clap as one dark column spanning every frequency at once.
