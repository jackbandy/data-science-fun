# Sources — `assets/week8`

NOTICE: this file was drafted by an LLM coding system and has not yet been reviewed.

Figures generated by `make_week8_figures.py`:

- `bias-variance-bullseye.svg` — synthetic; redrawn after the bulls-eye diagram in Scott Fortmann-Roe, ["Understanding the Bias-Variance Tradeoff"](https://web.archive.org/web/20130301064623/http://scott.fortmann-roe.com/docs/BiasVariance.html) (June 2012). No pixels copied; the layout (rows = bias, columns = variance) follows his figure.
- `loss-functions.svg` — plain math, no data. Squared and absolute loss as in Sam Lau, Joey Gonzalez, and Deb Nolan, [*Learning Data Science*](https://learningds.org/ch/04/modeling_loss_functions.html), §4.2.
- `house-average-loss.svg`, `overfit-data.svg`, `overfit-a.svg`, `overfit-ab.svg`, `overfit-abc.svg`, `overfit-abc-labeled.svg` — Cook County home sales 2025 from `datasets/cook-county-home-sales-2025/` in this repo, filtered as in the Week 5 slides.
- `house-plane-3d.svg` — Cook County home sales 2025 from `datasets/cook-county-home-sales-2025/`, filtered as in the Week 5 slides. Least squares plane for sale price on building sqft and bedrooms, fit on all sales; 300 sampled sales are drawn, with bedrooms jittered. Rendered with `surface_figure` from `../barocas-inscrutability/make_inscrutability_figures.py`, so the box matches the Week 5 Barocas figures. The 3D plane view follows Fig. 8.7 in Tiffany Timbers et al., [*Data Science: A First Introduction with Python*](https://python.datasciencebook.ca/regression2.html#multivariable-linear-regression), §8.6.
- `cls-*-scatter.svg`, `cls-*-four-boundaries.svg`, `cls-universities-line.svg`, `cls-universities-boundary.svg` — the five Day 2 classification examples, all from `datasets/classification-examples/` in this repo (sources and licenses in its README): College Scorecard (U.S. Department of Education, public domain); Spambase (Hopkins, Reeber, Forman, and Suermondt, UCI, [doi:10.24432/C53G6X](https://doi.org/10.24432/C53G6X), CC BY 4.0); Palmer Penguins (Horst, Hill, and Gorman, [doi:10.5281/zenodo.3960218](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.3960218), CC0; data from Gorman, Williams, and Fraser, *PLoS ONE* 2014); Breast Cancer Wisconsin (Diagnostic) (Wolberg, Mangasarian, Street, and Street, UCI, [doi:10.24432/C5DW2B](https://doi.org/10.24432/C5DW2B), CC BY 4.0); Cook County home sales 2025 (lot size) with 2020 census tract housing units per square mile (U.S. Census Bureau TIGERweb). Decision regions from scikit-learn's `DecisionTreeClassifier`, `GaussianNB`, `KNeighborsClassifier`, and `SVC(kernel="linear")`.
- `cls-penguins-split.svg`, `cls-penguins-validation.svg`, `cls-penguins-test.svg` — Palmer Penguins (Adélie and Gentoo) from `datasets/classification-examples/`, as above. Split 70/10/20 into training, validation, and test sets with scikit-learn's `train_test_split` (`random_state=418`, stratified by species). Validation panels are `KNeighborsClassifier` on standardized features with k = 1, 15, and 191, fit on the training set; the test panel refits k = 15 on training + validation.
- `cls-penguins-knn-intuition.svg` — the same training set; lines to the 5 nearest training penguins (Euclidean distance on standardized features) for two new bills, 45 × 15 mm and 45 × 17 mm. The build-up frames `cls-penguins-knn-intuition-data.svg`, `-1.svg`, `-1-vote.svg`, and `-2.svg` are the same figure with the data alone, then each new penguin added before its vote.
- `supervised-two-kinds.svg` — one synthetic dataset (`numpy` seed 418): 30 students' hours studied and exam scores (score = 45 + 5 × hours + noise). Above, a least squares line predicts the score; below, a linear `SVC` on hours predicts pass (score of 70 or more) or fail.
