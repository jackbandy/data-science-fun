## datasaurus-og.gif / datasaurus-og.png

The site-wide preview card (`og:image` / `twitter:image`, set in `docs/_config.yml`). A cloud
of orange points cycles between the Chicago Orange Line route and four of the Datasaurus
Dozen shapes — dino, star, circle, dots — returning to the route between each one. All five
share mean(x), mean(y), sd(x) and sd(y) to two decimals.

**Source of the Datasaurus data and of the idea:** Justin Matejka and George Fitzmaurice,
["Same Stats, Different Graphs: Generating Datasets with Varied Appearance and Identical Statistics through Simulated Annealing," *CHI 2017*](https://www.autodesk.com/research/publications/same-stats-different-graphs).
The `dino` dataset is Alberto Cairo's original Datasaurus. Data released by the authors under
CC BY-SA 4.0; it lives in this repo at `datasets/datasaurus-dozen/`.

The fifth shape, `lasso`, is not from the paper: it is the Orange Line route from
`docs/assets/orange-line/orange-line-trace.svg`, sampled at even arc length and z-scored onto
the dozen's own moments.

Generated by `docs/assets/orange-line/sandbox/make_datasaurus_gif_og.py`:

    python make_datasaurus_gif_og.py --width 1200 --poster --out ../../branding/datasaurus-og.gif

- **datasaurus-og.gif** — 1200 × 742, 224 frames at 25 fps, ~0.7 MB.
- **datasaurus-og.png** — first frame (the Orange Line), the fallback for scrapers with a
  size ceiling or no GIF support.
