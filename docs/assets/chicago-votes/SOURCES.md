# Sources — `chicago-votes`

> _Note: this file was drafted with an LLM._

A bar chart of Chicago's six citywide ballot questions from the November 3, 2026 general election, sorted by Yes share, showing where the final share could land once the ballots not yet counted are in. The outer limits are the worst cases (every uncounted ballot votes No, or every one votes Yes); between them, a Beta prior on the uncounted ballots' Yes share, peaked at the counted share, says which outcomes are likelier (`UNCOUNTED_CONCENTRATION` in the script, an uncalibrated assumption). Generated by `make_chicago_votes.py` (`python3 make_chicago_votes.py --fetch` re-downloads the report; needs Poppler's `pdftotext`).

## Data

### `data/cboe-summary-report.pdf`

The Chicago Board of Election Commissioners' [Summary Report (2 Column)](https://results.chicagoelections.gov/results/Summary%20Report%20%282%20Column%29.pdf), "Summary Report - Unofficial Results". The committed copy is "CHI 20261103 Ver D", data refreshed Wednesday, November 4, 2026 2:12 AM, with 1,232 of 1,291 precincts reported. The Board updates this file until results are certified, so `--fetch` overwrites it with the current version.

### `data/referenda.csv`

Written by `make_chicago_votes.py`: the six citywide questions parsed from the PDF (Yes, No, precincts reported), plus the estimated uncounted ballots, the current, lowest, and highest possible Yes share, and the 25% and 75% quantiles of the final share under the prior.

### Outstanding ballots

`OUTSTANDING_MAIL_BALLOTS` in the script is hand-copied from the Board's [Outstanding Ballot Count](https://chicagoelections.gov/outstanding-ballot-count) page, which refuses automated fetches. Read on 2026-11-04 from Safari: "Total Uncounted Ballots: 30,987", all of them "Vote By Mail – Sent, Not Returned Back to Board (Outstanding)". The page carries no timestamp, so re-read it before each re-run.

### Question labels

Short labels paraphrase the Board's contest titles, using the descriptions in Block Club Chicago's ["From Millionaires' Tax To Public Fairgrounds, You Can Vote On These Ballot Questions In November"](https://blockclubchicago.org/2026/10/14/from-millionaires-tax-to-public-fairgrounds-you-can-vote-on-these-ballot-questions-in-november/) (Oct 14, 2026). "ADVISORY QUESTION REGARDING MILLIONAIRES SURCHARGE" and "ADVISORY QUESTION REGARDING CHICAGO LAND OWNERS" are the Chicago Board of Education's questions; "The Cook County Millionaire Amendment for Property Tax Relief" is Cook County's.

## Design

- Bar color: Chicago flag light blue `#41B6E6`, from the [Wikimedia Commons flag SVG](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/9/9b/Flag_of_Chicago%2C_Illinois.svg). It is 2.26:1 against white, so every bar carries a direct value label in ink.
- Title: Big Shoulders Bold, the City of Chicago's municipal typeface per its [typography guidance](https://design.chicago.gov/typography/) ("Big Shoulders is the Municipal Typeface for the City of Chicago"). Everything else is Arial.
