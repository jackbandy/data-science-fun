# Sources — `viz-basics`

Every SVG in this folder is generated by `make_viz_basics_figures.py`; that
script is the source of truth. Re-run it after changing anything:

```bash
docs/slides/.venv/bin/python docs/assets/viz-basics/make_viz_basics_figures.py [name ...]
```

The figures accompany the Week 5 slides (`docs/slides/week5.qmd`). Nothing here
is third-party artwork — the *data* has outside provenance, listed below.

## Palettes

* Neutrals and the orange accent follow `docs/assets/STYLE.md`.
* `'L'` route colors are the official CTA values from the
  [CTA Trademark Guidelines for Developers](https://www.transitchicago.com/developers/branding/).
* `OKABE_ITO` is the eight-color qualitative palette from Masataka Okabe and
  Kei Ito, ["Color Universal Design"](https://jfly.uni-koeln.de/color/) (2008).
* `simulate_deuteranopia` implements Viénot, Brettel and Mollon, "Digital video
  colourbars for testing dichromatic colour vision," *Color Research &
  Application* 24:4 (1999), 243–252. It is used to *check* palettes, never to
  claim what any particular person sees.

## Fonts

Libre Franklin (OFL) from `docs/assets/fonts/`, drawn as paths so each SVG is
