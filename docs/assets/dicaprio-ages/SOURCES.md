# Sources — `dicaprio-ages`

> _Note: this file was drafted with an LLM. Data transcribed 2026-09-26. The original image is third-party copyrighted material, collected here for classroom discussion (fair use); do not redistribute._

`make_dicaprio_ages.py` writes `dicaprio-ages-blank.svg` (axes only) and `dicaprio-ages-complete.svg` (Leo's age as a line, his girlfriend's age that year as bars, with names).

## Data

`dicaprio-ages.csv` is transcribed, value for value, from the chart "Leonardo DiCaprio's Dating History" (credited to the *Daily Mail*), as posted to Reddit:

- u/Far-Building3569, "Leonardo DiCaprio's age contrasted with his girlfriends," r/charts, 14 Nov 2025: <https://www.reddit.com/r/charts/comments/1owzkul/leonardo_dicaprios_age_contrasted_with_his/> (post text: "SOURCE: Daily Mail"). Image: <https://i.redd.it/p7xw4auar81g1.jpeg>.

The ages are the chart's own, including its conventions (e.g. Leo is 24 in 1999, though he turned 25 that November; Camila Morrone goes 21 → 23 between 2019 and 2020).

The chart stops in 2022. The top comment on the post (Nov 2025): "His latest is now 27. Chart needs update."
