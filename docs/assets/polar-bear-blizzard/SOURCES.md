### 1. Polar Bear in a Blizzard

**File:** `polar-bear-in-a-blizzard-donohue.jpg` (JPEG, 2000 × 1331)
**Credit:** Photo © Carl Donohue / Skolai Images. All rights reserved.
**Caption (as published):** "A young male polar Bear walking across the ground in a heavy snowstorm, (Ursus maritimus), Arctic National Wildlife Refuge, ANWR, Alaska."

A white subject against a white background in falling snow: the bear is there in every pixel, but it takes work — and the right contrast — to see it. Used in the Week 2 deck as the picture of signal buried in noise, the thing descriptive statistics and plots are for.
