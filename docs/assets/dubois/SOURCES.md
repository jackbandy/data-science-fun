# W.E.B. Du Bois Data Visualizations

Hand-drawn charts created by W.E.B. Du Bois and students of Atlanta University for "The Exhibit of American Negroes" at the 1900 Paris Exposition Universelle. All images are in the public domain (no known restrictions on publication) and are sourced from the Library of Congress Prints and Photographs Division, collection "American Negro Exhibit of the American Section at the Paris Exposition Universelle, 1900" (ANEDUB).

---

## Images

### 1. City and Rural Population. 1890
**File:** `city-and-rural-population-1890.jpg`  
**Source:** https://www.loc.gov/pictures/collection/anedub/item/2013650430/  
**Direct image:** https://tile.loc.gov/storage-services/service/pnp/ppmsca/33800/33873v.jpg  
**LoC reproduction no.:** LC-DIG-ppmsca-33873  
A spiral chart showing the proportion of Georgia's Black population living in cities vs. rural areas. The tight red spiral at the bottom represents 734,952 people living in the country and villages vs. 78,139 in large cities.
