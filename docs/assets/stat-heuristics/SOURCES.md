## freq-stat-heuristics-tree.png

A decision-tree flowchart for choosing a frequentist statistical test

**Source: this is Figure 1.1 of Richard McElreath, _Statistical
Rethinking: A Bayesian Course with Examples in R and Stan_, 2nd ed. (Boca Raton: CRC Press, 2020), chapter 1, "The Golem of Prague."**

- Companion: https://bookdown.org/wade_vanderwright/SR_2022_bookdown/
- Figure file: https://bookdown.org/wade_vanderwright/SR_2022_bookdown/_images/fig1.1.png

The figure is McElreath's own illustration of the "pre-made test from a flowchart" approach that chapter 1 argues *against* — it is not presented as a recommended procedure.

The underlying statistical relationships are textbook material, but this particular arrangement, wording, and box layout are McElreath's.
