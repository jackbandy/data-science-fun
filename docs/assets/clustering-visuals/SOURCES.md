# Clustering Visuals — Sources

> _Note: this file and the figures were drafted by an LLM coding system and have not yet been reviewed._

All figures are generated by `make_clustering_figures.py` (`python3 make_clustering_figures.py [name ...]`). Random choices use fixed seeds, so the figures regenerate identically. Used in the Week 10 slides (k-means, hierarchical clustering, and DBSCAN on Day 1; cluster evaluation on Day 2); the karate-club figures are used in the Week 12 slides (community detection).

## Data

- **Palmer Penguins** (`penguins-labels-*`, `kmeans-penguins-*`, `kmedoids-penguins-*`, `kmeans-restarts-*`, `ffh-penguins-*`, `elbow-penguins-*`, `scaling-penguins-*`, `dendrogram-penguins-*`, `silhouette-penguins`, `choose-k-*`, `contingency-penguins`) — `datasets/classification-examples/penguins.csv`, the 342 penguins with all four measurements. Allison Horst, Alison Hill, and Kristen Gorman, [palmerpenguins](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.3960218) (2020), CC0; data originally from Kristen B. Gorman, Tony D. Williams, and William R. Fraser, *PLoS ONE* 9(3): e90081 (2014), <https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0090081>. Bill length vs. flipper length, standardized, follows *Data Science: A First Introduction* (Python ed.), [ch. 9](https://python.datasciencebook.ca/clustering.html).
- **CTA 'L' stations** (`l-stations-*`, `l-kmeans`, `l-linkage-*`, `l-kdist`, `l-dbscan-*`) — `datasets/chicago-l-stations/`, from the City of Chicago Data Portal, [CTA System Information: List of 'L' Stops](https://data.cityofchicago.org/Transportation/CTA-System-Information-List-of-L-Stops/8pix-ypme). One point per station (`MAP_ID`, 144 stations), projected to kilometers. `l-stations-v1` uses the official route colors per the [CTA Trademark Guidelines for Developers](https://www.transitchicago.com/developers/branding/); no CTA marks are used.
- **CTA ridership** (`l-ridership-*`) — `datasets/cta-ridership/Station_Entries_-_Daily_Totals_20260527.csv`, from the City of Chicago Data Portal, [CTA Ridership: 'L' Station Entries, Daily Totals](https://data.cityofchicago.org/Transportation/CTA-Ridership-L-Station-Entries-Daily-Totals/5neh-572f). Six 2025 features per station, using medians: typical weekday entries (log), Saturday ÷ weekday, Sunday ÷ weekday, summer ÷ winter weekday, 99th-percentile day ÷ median day, and 2025 ÷ 2019 weekday. Standardized, k-means with K = 4; cluster names are ours, assigned from each cluster's profile. 141 of the 144 stations; Damen-Lake, Lawrence, and Berwyn lack a usable 2025 or 2019 baseline.
- **Movie reviews** (`reviews-clusters-*`) — `datasets/movie-reviews/reviews.csv`, 4,500 Amazon reviews (Hou et al., "Bridging Language and Items for Retrieval and Recommendation: Benchmarking LLMs as Semantic Encoders," [arXiv:2403.03952](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.03952), 2024; Amazon Reviews 2023). TF-IDF on words and two-word phrases (English stop words removed, `&#34;` decoded), truncated SVD to 100 dimensions, k-means with K = 6. The sentiment labels are used only to color the reveal.
- **Karate club** (`karate-club-*`) — Wayne W. Zachary, "An Information Flow Model for Conflict and Fission in Small Groups," *Journal of Anthropological Research* 33(4): 452–473 (1977), <https://doi.org/10.1086/jar.33.4.3629752>; the unweighted 34-member, 78-tie version packaged as `networkx.karate_club_graph()`. Split in two by the sign of the graph Laplacian's second eigenvector.
- **Opinion lexicon** (`reviews-words-*`) — `datasets/movie-reviews/opinion-lexicon/`, Bing Liu's [Opinion Lexicon](https://www.cs.uic.edu/~liub/FBS/sentiment-analysis.html) (2,006 positive and 4,783 negative words), CC BY 4.0, first published with Minqing Hu and Bing Liu, "Mining and Summarizing Customer Reviews," *KDD 2004*: 168–177, <https://doi.org/10.1145/1014052.1014073>; copied from the [NLTK data](https://github.com/nltk/nltk_data) mirror.
- **Ashland photo** (`color-compression-v*`, `.jpg`/`.png`) — Pi.1415926535, ["Entrance to Ashland station, December 2018"](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Entrance_to_Ashland_station,_December_2018.JPG), CC BY-SA 3.0, via Wikimedia Commons (the slides' Week 10 background, `orange-line-stops-better/stop11-ashland-a.jpg`). Resized to 960 px wide and recolored by k-means in CIELAB (K = 64, 32, 16, 8, 4, 2; fit on 20,000 sampled pixels). **These derived images are licensed CC BY-SA 3.0, like the original**, not under the repo's default license.
- **Skyline photo** (`color-compression-skyline-v*`, `.jpg`/`.png`) — Warren LeMay, ["View from Chicago River at Park No. 571, Eleanor Street, Bridgeport, Chicago, IL"](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:View_from_Chicago_River_at_Park_No._571,_Eleanor_Street,_Bridgeport,_Chicago,_IL_(54832903478).jpg), CC BY-SA 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons (`orange-line-stops-better/stop11-ashland-b.jpg`). Same recoloring as above. **These derived images are licensed CC BY-SA 4.0, like the original.**
- **Stations in 2-D and 3-D** (`l-ridership-2d-*`, `l-ridership-3d-*`) — the same station features as `l-ridership-*`, using only typical weekday and Saturday ÷ weekday (2-D), then adding busiest ÷ typical day (3-D); k-means, K = 4 each time. Marker shapes are matched to the six-feature groups by largest overlap.
- **Reviews in 2-D** (`reviews-2d-*`) — the same TF-IDF features, truncated SVD to 3 directions, plotted and clustered (K = 3) on directions 2 and 3; direction 1 mostly tracks how much common vocabulary a review uses.
- **Reviews by sentiment words** (`reviews-words-*`) — each review's count of opinion-lexicon words, positive and negative (lowercased, `&#34;` decoded, whole-word matches only, no handling of negation such as "not good"); k-means, K = 3, on log(1 + count). One dot per pair of counts, area growing with the number of reviews. The third circled example is an excerpt of a longer review; its counts are for the whole review.
- **Karate embedding** (`karate-embed-*`) — each member placed at its entries in the graph Laplacian's 2nd and 3rd eigenvectors; the split is the sign of the 2nd (the Fiedler vector), the same split shown in `karate-club-v1`.
- **Condo sales** (`condos-dbscan-*`) — `datasets/cook-county-home-sales-2025/`, from the Cook County Assessor's Office via Cook County Open Data ([Parcel Sales](https://datacatalog.cookcountyil.gov/Property-Taxation/Assessor-Parcel-Sales/wvhk-k5uv), [Parcel Universe](https://datacatalog.cookcountyil.gov/Property-Taxation/Assessor-Parcel-Universe/nj4t-kc8j)). The 14,088 condominium sales of one parcel each (`num_parcels_sale == 1`): bulk sales repeat one row per unit at one location, so a single transaction would otherwise form its own cluster. Coordinates are rounded to 3 decimals (about 110 m) by the dataset, so some sales stack exactly. Projected to kilometers from the mean location; DBSCAN with MinPts = 40 and Eps = 0.1, 0.2, 0.3, 0.5, and 1.0 km. Labels name each of the three biggest clusters by its most common Chicago community area (or mailing city outside Chicago), plus the count of other areas holding at least 2% of its sales. Dots are rasterized inside the SVG to keep it small.
- **Synthetic / hand-placed** — `kmedoids-v*`, `linkage-*`, `agglomerative-steps-*`, `dbscan-points-*`, `dbscan-walk-*`, `cohesion-separation-*`, and `cluster-shapes` (scikit-learn's `make_blobs`, `make_moons`, `make_circles`, plus two Gaussian clouds).

## Ideas after

- `cluster-shapes` — after the scikit-learn ["Comparing different clustering algorithms on toy datasets"](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/auto_examples/cluster/plot_cluster_comparison.html) figure, as used in Elena Zheleva's CS 418 slides (Mar. 10, 2026). Redrawn with our own data and parameters.
- `linkage-*`, `dbscan-points-*`, `cohesion-separation-*`, `agglomerative-steps-*` — the definitions follow Gonzalo Bello Lander, CS 418 Lectures 14 and 15 ("Clustering (I)" and "(II)", 2026). The points are ours.
- `kmeans-penguins-*` — the assign/refit steps follow Zheleva (Mar. 10, 2026) and Bello Lecture 15.
