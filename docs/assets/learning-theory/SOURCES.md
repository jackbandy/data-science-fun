## zpd-loop-v0..v4.svg + zpd-loop-stack.md (remix)

- **Files:** `zpd-loop-v0.svg` … `zpd-loop-v4.svg` and `zpd-loop-stack.md`, all
  generated by `make_zpd_loop_figure.py` in this folder — a build in which the
  labels accumulate one at a time: v0 blank, v1 adds the innermost (unaided),
  v2 adds the outermost (cannot do), v3 adds the middle (the zone) and so
  carries all three, v4 rings the zone in white.
- **Two outputs:** the `.svg` files stand alone with fonts embedded;
  `zpd-loop-stack.md` is a Quarto include holding the same five frames as inline
  SVG for the "Zone of Proximal Development" slide in `../../slides/week1.qmd`.
  Reveal scales `.slides` with a CSS transform and an `<img>` is rasterised at
  its layout size beforehand, so an `<img>` figure goes soft; inline SVG stays
  vector. The inline copies carry no `@font-face` and inherit the deck's
  Libre Franklin.
- **Derived from:** `Zone_of_proximal_development.svg` above (Dcoetzee, CC0)
- **License:** CC0 — the source is public domain, so this remix carries no
  further restriction; credit to Dcoetzee is courtesy, not a licence condition.
- **Changes:** three nested circles become three nested rounded rectangles,
  carrying the Loop's corner radius (5.7% of width, read off
  `../orange-line/loop-icon.svg`); the frame is a portrait golden rectangle that
  the outermost band fills; the blue-violet ramp becomes three steps of the brand
  orange, keeping the original's light-outside / dark-inside ordering; labels are
  white bold Libre Franklin over a black drop shadow. The original's
  parenthetical glosses moved to the slide's bullets — at the size this figure is
  shown they were unreadable however crisply they rendered.
